1. Salaar

Salaar: Part 1 – Ceasefire is a 2023 Indian action film directed by Prashanth Neel and starring Prabhas and Prithviraj Sukumaran. The story revolves around Deva, a powerful and loyal man, and his childhood friend Varadharaja Mannar. Their friendship becomes complicated because of political struggles, violence, and a battle for power in the fictional kingdom of Khansaar. The movie is known for its intense action sequences, dark atmosphere, powerful characters, and strong background music. It focuses heavily on friendship, loyalty, betrayal, and the struggle for power.

2. KGF 2

KGF: Chapter 2 is a 2022 Indian action film directed by Prashanth Neel and starring Yash, Sanjay Dutt, Srinidhi Shetty, and Raveena Tandon. It continues the story of Rocky, who has established himself as the powerful ruler of the Kolar Gold Fields. Rocky faces several enemies, including Adheera and political forces that want to take control of KGF. The movie focuses on ambition, power, revenge, loyalty, and Rocky's determination to fulfill his promises. It is known for its large-scale action sequences, dramatic storytelling, powerful dialogues, and Yash's portrayal of Rocky.

3. Kantara Chapter 1

Kantara: Chapter 1, commonly referred to as Kantara 2, is the prequel to the 2022 film Kantara. It is directed by and stars Rishab Shetty. The movie explores the historical and mythological background of the world and traditions shown in the first Kantara. It focuses on the relationship between people, nature, culture, spirituality, and divine traditions. The film is particularly notable for its connection to the folklore and traditions of coastal Karnataka. Rishab Shetty has also been involved in developing the story and direction, making the film an important part of the Kantara universe.
