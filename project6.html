01 — The shortlist

Not just places.
Different versions of you.

A handpicked five for the traveler who wants a little bit of everything: wonder, color, calm, adventure and a good excuse to disappear for a while.

Agra · Uttar Pradesh
01 / Wonder

Taj Mahal

For the hopeless romantics.

White marble, reflecting pools and that moment when the monument appears at the end of the garden. Come early, slow down and let one of India's most iconic sights do the talking.

History Architecture Sunrise Romance

Goa · West Coast
02 / Slow down

Goa

For the ones who packed no plans.

Warm sand, palms, beach shacks and sunsets that make you forget what time it is. Goa is the place to trade your schedule for sea breeze and one more evening outside.

Beaches Food Sunsets Good times

Kerala · South India
03 / Breathe

Kerala

For slow mornings and greener days.

Drift through palm-fringed backwaters, wake up on a houseboat, wander through lush landscapes and let the pace of the trip become wonderfully, deliberately slow.

Backwaters Houseboats Nature Wellness

Jaipur · Rajasthan
04 / Get lost

Jaipur

For color, culture and royal energy.

Pink streets, grand palaces, tiny craft shops and food worth getting lost for. Jaipur turns a simple walk into a visual adventure around every corner.

Palaces Culture Shopping Food

Ladakh · Himalayas
05 / Go further

Ladakh

For the ones who want the road to matter.

High passes, enormous skies, stark mountains and roads that seem to lead straight into the horizon. Ladakh is less about ticking sights off a list and more about remembering the journey.
